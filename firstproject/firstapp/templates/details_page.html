UI/UX Design Tutorial

Here are some key principles to keep in mind when designing modern, user-friendly interfaces:

  1. Keep it simple and focused.
  2. Make sure the design is consistent.
  3. Prioritise usability and accessibility.
  4. Use clear visual hierarchy.
  5. Test with real users and iterate.

Great design isn’t just about how it looks,
it’s about how it works. ✨
